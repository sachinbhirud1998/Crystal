# Risk register

## Purpose

Track risks that could affect delivery, reliability, security, cost, or supportability of Project Crystal.

## Scope

Use this template for a milestone, domain, or change. Issues already occurring should be tracked separately as incidents or delivery blockers.

## Background

Risk ownership and review prevent assumptions from becoming unmanaged exposure.

## Standards and best practices

Use evidence-based likelihood and impact, assign one accountable owner per risk, and record residual risk after mitigation. Escalate risks that exceed agreed tolerance through the milestone sign-off process.
