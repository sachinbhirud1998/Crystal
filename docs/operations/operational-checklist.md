# Operational readiness checklist

## Purpose

Use this checklist to confirm a planned change can be owned, observed, supported, and recovered safely.

## Scope

Complete it before production-affecting work or when required by the milestone owner. It is a template, not an approval by itself.

## Background

Operational readiness is established through explicit ownership and evidence, not assumptions about an implementation.

## Checklist

- [ ] Accountable owner and escalation path are recorded.
- [ ] Support boundaries and affected environments are documented.
- [ ] Relevant runbook and troubleshooting guidance exists or is scheduled.
- [ ] Validation criteria and evidence locations are defined.
- [ ] Rollback trigger, safe stop point, and decision owner are identified.
- [ ] Security and cost reviews are complete or have approved exceptions.
- [ ] Monitoring and alerting needs are assessed for a future authorized implementation.
- [ ] Stakeholders and change communication expectations are documented.

## Standards and best practices

Use links to durable repository records, keep evidence free of secrets, and record any unchecked item as a risk with an owner and due date.
