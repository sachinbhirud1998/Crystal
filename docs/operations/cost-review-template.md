# Cost review

## Purpose

Record the expected cost impact, assumptions, constraints, and approval for a proposed platform change.

## Scope

Use before authorizing work that may affect recurring or one-time spend. This record supports the project budget; it is not a financial forecast or purchase approval.

## Background

Project Crystal is designed to remain within a constrained learning budget. Cost decisions must remain visible as architecture and delivery choices evolve.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Scope and usage assumptions are documented.
- [ ] Expected spend is compared with the approved budget.
- [ ] Cost drivers, limits, and shutdown or cleanup expectations are identified.
- [ ] Lower-cost alternatives were considered where practical.
- [ ] Cost ownership and review cadence are assigned.
- [ ] Material uncertainty or variance is recorded as a risk.

## Standards and best practices

Use conservative assumptions, distinguish estimates from observed cost, and update the record when scope changes. Never include account identifiers or billing exports containing sensitive data.
