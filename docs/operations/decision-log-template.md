# Decision log

## Purpose

Capture working decisions that guide delivery but do not meet the threshold for an Architecture Decision Record.

## Scope

Use for time-bound, local, or low-consequence decisions. Escalate material architecture, security, cost, or operating-model decisions to an ADR.

## Background

A concise decision log preserves context, avoids repeated debate, and makes assumptions visible during review.
